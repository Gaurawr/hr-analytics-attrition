# 👥 HR Analytics - Employee Attrition Analysis

## Overview
Analysis of IBM HR Employee Attrition dataset to identify 
key factors causing employee turnover.

## Tools Used
- 🗄️ SQL — Data querying and analysis
- 📊 Power BI — Interactive dashboard
- 📁 CSV — Data source (IBM HR Dataset)

## Key Insights
- Total Employees: 1,470
- Attrition Count: 237 (16.1% attrition rate)
- Average Age: 36.92 years
- Average Monthly Income: $6,502
- Sales department has highest attrition
- Age group 25-35 most likely to leave
- Low salary = High attrition risk

## Dashboard Features
- KPI Cards — Employees, Attrition, Age, Income
- Attrition by Department
- Attrition by Job Role
- Age group analysis
- Income vs Attrition correlation

## Business Impact
This analysis helps HR teams:
- Identify high risk employees
- Reduce hiring costs
- Improve retention strategies
